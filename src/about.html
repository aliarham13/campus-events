About Campus Events

Connecting students, ideas, and opportunities through engaging campus activities.

Who We Are

Campus Events is a student-driven initiative dedicated to organizing meaningful, enjoyable, and impactful activities throughout the academic year. From tech fests and cultural nights to workshops and sports tournaments, our goal is to make campus life vibrant and memorable.

We work closely with student societies, departments, and administration to provide a structured platform for planning and promoting events, ensuring that every student has access to opportunities for learning, networking, and fun.

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

  • Encourage student participation and leadership through events.
  • Provide a centralized platform for event discovery and registration.
  • Promote a culture of collaboration among societies and departments.
  • Support both academic and co-curricular growth of students.

Core Values

  • Inclusivity
  • Creativity
  • Collaboration
  • Professionalism

What We Do

We help with:

  • Event planning & coordination
  • Promotion & registrations
  • Venue & logistics support
  • Photography & coverage coordination

Our Team Structure

Different roles working together to deliver smooth, well-organized events.

Event Coordinator

Oversees event planning, approvals, and communication between societies and administration.

Logistics & Operations

Manages venues, equipment, schedules, and on-ground arrangements.

Media & Promotions

Handles posters, social media, announcements, and event coverage.

Student Volunteers

Support event execution, registrations, and coordination on event day.
